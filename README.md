# washroom.status.usa

developing Washroom Status USA, a platform that helps people across the United States find nearby public restrooms and check their cleanliness, accessibility, and availability.
The main goal is not just to help people locate restrooms, but also to create public accountability so gas stations, restaurants, and other businesses are encouraged to maintain clean and hygienic facilities.
Core Features:

Nearby Restroom Finder: GPS-based interactive map displaying nearby public restrooms.
Restroom Availability: Show whether restrooms are open, closed, temporarily unavailable, or unverified.
Cleanliness Ratings: Allow users to rate restrooms from 1–5 stars.
Clean or Dirty Status: Clearly categorize restrooms as Excellent, Clean, Average, Poor, or Very Dirty based on recent reviews.
Photo Reviews: Allow users to upload photos and report problems such as dirty floors, broken toilets, bad odors, or missing supplies.
Worst-Rated Restrooms: Publicly display consistently low-rated restrooms based on sufficient credible reviews. This should encourage businesses to improve their facilities.
Best-Rated Restrooms: Highlight businesses maintaining excellent hygiene.
Business Owner Dashboard: Allow owners to respond to complaints, address problems, and share updates.
Improvement Tracking: Show whether a restroom’s cleanliness has improved or declined over time.
Accessibility Filters: Wheelchair accessibility, gender-neutral restrooms, baby-changing facilities, free access, and 24/7 availability.
Community Reporting: Allow users to add new restroom locations and update outdated information.
Admin Dashboard: Manage listings, moderate reviews, verify reports, and prevent fake ratings.

Important Development Requirements:

Use real restroom location data from reliable sources such as OpenStreetMap.
Prioritize mobile-first design and fast loading.
Start with one US city before expanding nationwide.
Use timestamps and review counts to make ratings transparent.
Prevent fake reviews, harassment, and unfair business rankings.
Protect restroom users’ privacy when accepting photographs.
Never claim real-time availability without verified information.
Provide business owners an opportunity to respond and demonstrate improvements.
Do not use fake or demo data in the publicly launched product.
Suggested Technology:
